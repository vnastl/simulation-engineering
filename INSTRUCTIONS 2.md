This is our study of interest:
https://datacatalogue.ukdataservice.ac.uk/studies/study/5723#details

The documentation files from the webpage are in downloads. The data is stored in the zip files. They were transformed from raw survey data + a PDF codebook to a persona according to CODEBOOK_TO_TEXT_PLAYBOOK.md.
Every stage should be rendered as a single self-contained HTML file according to TRACE_PLAYBOOK.md.

1) Check the project webpage of the study and related papers in the folder publications.

2) Follow the instructions in TASK_PLAYBOOK.md to add micro- and macro-level simulation tasks on top of the personas — prompts with scoring rubrics and data-derived answer keys, grounded in the related papers.

3) Follow the instructions in TRACE_PLAYBOOK.md to walk one record through your added pipeline.
