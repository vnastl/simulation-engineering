**Subject —** Well, I'd already been interested – well through school, I'd always been interested in Art and I'd always wanted to do something with that and then I got to college and I did design and technology and I did really enjoy that as well and I thought doing the technology will be using my brain more than the arts – you know people always say oh I can't get a job in a art, so I thought oh, I'll take that course then. And I do still enjoy you know, some arty aspects, and I do try and get – you know – sort of that into my work. Yeah, I decided – I was either going to be a graphic designer or it was just, you know, design technology and talking to my tutors and everything and they said, oh well maybe you should go for the design technology then it's a bit – it's not – it's still a bit creative but you can use your brain in other aspects of the stuff rather than going for a tot – you know graphic design – it's hard to get a job in those sort of areas. So then I decided on industrial design in the end. I didn't quite realise how much technology would be involved though, 'cos like on the open day they said – I said, oh well I did Maths at GCSE, and I got an A for that, and so will I be OK? And they said, oh yeah, if you get the C at GCSE you're fine and I got here and it was really difficult, I found it really hard. And a lot of people did. I think a few of the people did drop out in the first year because, it didn't seem to be specified that – you know actually that you'd be having electronics every week and then you swap over and do mechanics and you'll have tests on these and then. Yeah, I did Ok on some of the mechanics stuff, 'cos I didn't – well, they teach you, you know – taught that quite well, but some of the electronics I did really struggle on that, so it did sort of put me off the course but then I thought I'll stick with it and see, you know, if it gets easier and obviously this year I didn't have to take that – I chose, you know, other aspects that I preferred so…

**Interviewer —** Did your family influence your decision at all?

**Subject —** Yeah, they did sort of because my Mum did say well, maybe you should do industrial design, instead of graphic design because it's – you know – quite difficult to get a job in graphics design. Yet with something like industrial design, because there's a lot of different areas you can go into then, they said you're probably better to do that.
