**Subject —** Yeah. If I wasn't on the placement, then they didn't want to sponsor me. Which is fair enough – they don't want to give me money if I'm not gonna work for them.

**Interviewer —** Were they quite reasonable with you about that then?

**Subject —** Yeah, it was – it was quite a joint decision between er the kind of advisor that I had that it sort of – I hadn't – I'd enjoyed working but I didn't kind of enjoy the environment that the company had. And I know, from other students who are sponsored with them, they don't like working for them either, but they want to do their placements, so I think it was sort of 50/50, I suppose.
