## Route into engineering — why they chose it, and who influenced them
*Coded variables informed by this theme (6): interest, family, varied, careers, teacher, school*

**Interviewer —** OK, what do your parents do, are either of them involved in the industry at all?

**Subject —** No, my brother is – my brother's doing the same thing but my parents aren't. My dad's more of a manager.

**Interviewer —** Is he? So when and why do you think that you decided to do design & technology?

**Subject —** Erm, it was always one of my better subjects at school, that I actually enjoyed – I got good marks on, that's why I carried on.

**Interviewer —** OK, what sort of things appealed to you about it when you were at school?

**Subject —** I think probably because it's more sort of – I think, I have – I think it's just the creativity – like, that you can do, you're not just stuck at you know, sort of writing an essay you had to get to you know – there are several aspects, you're not just stuck doing one thing. You get to do model making, you can go and do rendering, there's loads of things you can actually do.

**Interviewer —** OK, yeah, that's fine. Do you think it encouraged you, the thought that your brother was doing a similar course?

**Subject —** Erm, I think it helped yeah, because erm my family have always been sort of Art & Design, sort of like you know, with my parents, we went round galleries and things like that and to see exhibitions and stuff, so I think that's probably encouraged me a bit.

**Interviewer —** OK, did you do much career research or did you have any careers advice and stuff while you were at school?

**Subject —** No, I mean my technology teacher did the same thing and actually came to [this university] and he talked to me a bit about the background and what it was like but other than that, not really. I mean I did things like the work – the work experience at school but that was like in an office for like a couple of days. Erm, so I had that sort of view on it but that it was it really.

**Interviewer —** OK, so how did you decide to come to [this] University?

**Subject —** Er, because – well, I was told it was supposed to be the best university for this course. That's why. There were several universities and I think [university] had the highest points, that's why I put [university] first. Because of it's like reputation, because I thought well, you know, obviously aim for the highest one, if not then go for one with lower points.

## The industrial placement — whether and why to go (or not)
*Coded variables informed by this theme (4): placemen, wkexp, breaked, nogain*

**Interviewer —** The project that we're working on is looking at women in engineering and we've decided to include design and technology students in that as well, for two reasons really: one because we are also working with [name] University, and the design and technology side falls under engineering there (right) and then we're kind of looking at engineering as a male dominated environment, so we're looking at design and technology, to see if it's the same, that's the context and we're also looking at women's experiences of education so far and why they want to do a placement and what they're expecting to get out of placement and are you doing the placement?

**Subject —** No I'm not.

**Interviewer —** OK, why did you decide not to go on a placement?

**Subject —** I think it's more of my style of study – if I take a year out, I don't know if I'd – I'd find it really difficult to get back into the flow of working again, 'cos after the length of the summer holiday I find it really difficult getting back into the flow of like you know having to deal with a lot of course work and that. So that's really what it is. I just don't want to get out of the uni system.

**Interviewer —** Yeah, absolutely. Do you think that there would have been any advantages to doing a placement?

**Subject —** Er, not from what I've seen – like people that I know that have done placements and they're still struggling to find jobs. But I think it obviously depends on if you – if you're settled on placement and you know, they might offer you a job afterwards but from the people I've seen it's been a struggle for them whether they've had a placement or not.

Location – I'm not really too bothered about and training opportunities I think, to begin with I wouldn't mind if there weren't any training opportunities but then after, you know after a while, I'll be able to see what options there are.

**Subject —** My ideal career? Erm, well I think it's photography, which isn't actually what I'm doing, but yeah, I think photography. But because obviously, it's so difficult to get into and also there aren't many decent photography jobs available that's why I did design.

**Interviewer —** OK, do you think that you'll go into the design industry then?

**Subject —** I think I will but I'm not sure if it's – you know – which form to go into. I think I will go into the design industry but I don't have a lot of set thing of, oh you know, I want to work here, I want to do that because I haven't decided on that yet.
