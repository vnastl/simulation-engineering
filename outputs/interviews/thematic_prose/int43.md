## Route into engineering — why they chose it, and who influenced them
*Coded variables informed by this theme (14): highsal, special, mathsci, knowledg, family, hobbies, gooddeg, varied, mother, father, careers, teacher, discoura, school*

**Interviewer —** OK so why did you decide to do engineering?

I said OK, I bet you, I don’t, so I’ll do physics if I get A’s. So – I wanted to do Psychology, but er – I went to see a careers advisor and she said, have you thought of engineering? And I was like, I don’t even know what that is and she says well, it’s like when people make things that you know – they – that people use and things like that. And I said, that sounds really interesting and then I went on a – do you know Head Start? – I don’t suppose you’ve heard of Head Start, it’s like an engineering kind of - seventeen year olds getting experience kind of thing.

**Interviewer —** (I’ve heard of?) different things that are on the same lines, yeah.

**Subject —** Well, I kind of – I don’t know how I got into it, but I signed up for that so in the middle of like 2 years, I went on a week long finding out about engineering thing in Colorado. Erm, and that was really good, so you chose 2 – you wanted to look at and then you did a 3rd day and looked around all the other ones (yes). So the first day, I looked at civil, which is the one I kind of knew a bit about. Second day I looked at mechanical and the third day I looked at physics – doing actual physics – aero and electrical and I came out with liking civil the best really. Erm, I liked the management aspect best, so I just applied and that’s why really. And I couldn’t really think of anything else I wanted to do. I always wanted to be a PE teacher but you know, there’s not a lot to do with PE, is there really (laughter) And here I am. (OK). It was quite general as well, so I thought well – you know, and I’ve heard you can get jobs in other fields as well if you do engineering, so …

**Interviewer —** So the biggest influence were the careers people, would you say?

**Subject —** Probably. Also, in my physics class, everyone else was doing an engineering course. They wanted to do at Uni – so they were quite a big influence as well, and my Physics teacher as well – she’s a civil engineer. Erm, careers advisors kind of put the idea in my head and I had a – like went on research on my own and stuff. My parents said they didn’t have a clue.

**Interviewer —** OK, what do you think it is that actually appeals to you about the engineering then?

**Subject —** Hmm, I don’t know. I mean I – I would be lying if I said I hadn’t thought, I’m a girl and therefore I’ll get a job – because I have. That’s like – you know, that’s kind of like up there. I don’t know – I kind of like the engineering – the way it’s so general and you know you can go into consulting or you can go into like contracting, you can go on site, you can work in a design office, you can work even higher than that. It’s just so general – there’s so many aspects to building – I mean (lost words). I mean like you’ve got so many fields – you’ve got trains, roads, dams, bridges, you know. There’s so much stuff you can do – and that’s – that appealed to me a lot. My mate’s doing aero and all she does is aeroplanes. I mean it’s what she loves but it seems a bit narrow.

## Experience of the engineering degree and learning environment
*Coded variables informed by this theme (12): practica, pleased, relevanc, competit, assess, interper, supppers, groupwk, friends, theory, practwk, designwk*

**Interviewer —** What characteristics do you think make a good engineer?

**Subject —** Extremely practical, I’d say – because we’ve just had the hardest semester and I’ve got so many stupid – well, there probably are reasons for doing maths, but we’ve got this module called hydraulics and I think one person out of the whole class is going to go into this field and it just seems so – some of the modules are just so specific. I just wish we had more practical modules like actually like – that involve what we’re going to be doing because most people are going to be Site Managers or like Construction Engineers and things like that and I just wish it was more practical, but on the degree you get the theoretical side.

**Interviewer —** OK, what do you think has been the best and worst things about the course so far?

**Subject —** Best things – well I think the worst things is definitely the class exams, because they were so hard, and I worked really hard for them and I only got 54% and I was like – I’m not really happy about it because I’m on the MEng only if you get 60. Erm, that’s just – it’s just difficult when people erm, when you work really hard for things and you get the same as you would have done – if you know what I mean – when you’ve got guys who get like 70’s and they’re all like, oh I wish I’d done better. And you’re like – cabin packed? And – but, as I said before, I think the people that are more practical are probably the people who don’t do so well in these exams. They are so theoretical – well that’s a bad thing isn’t it? Yeah, good thing. I do go off the subject – I’m rambling.

**Interviewer —** How much course work have you had to do?

**Subject —** Not much actually. This semester we’ve only had like 20% for each module. So that’s 80% exams, so if you do crap in the exams, which a few people do, then it really buggers you up because I did OK on the coursework – I got like A’s and B’s and it’s just so – it’s just that I wish they’d been worth more, really. Which is a bit upsetting, but what you gonna do? The best thing is probably – I do enjoy the 100% course work like modules because we had one last year – it was really good. It was engineering design, it was group work but it was so good. It was just we had to design, this like building, and we did everything for it and the structure and all the – it was just really good, it was really fun – 100% all the way.

**Interviewer —** Yeah.

**Subject —** My friends probably. The guys on my course. If they say – if I say I think I’m not going to a tutorial, they go well I think I’m gonna go and I go, oh well I think I’ll go as well then. You know what I mean? But – it’s quite competitive on the course. You know, people know where people are in levels to themselves. So you don’t want to fall behind – you know what I mean? So yeah, definitely my mates on the course.

## The industrial placement — whether and why to go (or not)
*Coded variables informed by this theme (9): placemen, experien, money, jobprosp, jobdecid, industry, indchart, placothe, wkexp*

**Interviewer —** Do you think that university could do anything or do you think that it is too diverse?

**Subject —** Bit too diverse – way to diverse. I mean come back people have been working on trains and some people have been working in hospitals – there’s no way you can prepare for all of it. Also a lot of it’s like practical stuff, you know what I mean? There’s no way 2 years of – I guess – well in fact, I’ve learnt more in that summer than I did in the whole year of teaching and it’s just – I don’t think they can do it any other way really.

**Interviewer —** Why did you decide to take the sponsorship route?

**Subject —** So I could have some money.

**Interviewer —** Any other reasons?

**Subject —** No, not really. So I could get my foot in the door when I leave, without having to do interviews and things.

**Interviewer —** Do you think that there are any disadvantages?

**Subject —** Well, I’m now tied down. That’s the big problem. And also they got – they got off – they interviewed us after our first exams when we were – we’d just been at uni 4 months and they kept asking me questions like do you want to go for a job in contracting – I don’t know what on earth that means, you know? So they kind of get you when you don’t really know if you’ve had enough information to think well, actually I don’t want to go (?) I want to go in that (?). Sometimes it doesn’t sponsor. So, I think they should have got us a bit later maybe, like after the first year but I’m not bothered now. It’s alright.

**Interviewer —** So what sorts of things are you expecting to get out of the placement?

**Subject —** Well more idea about what I’m doing when I leave – like what field I want to go into – if any. Cos you can ask to be put into like 3 different places, depending on time of year – maybe that one, 3 months long (lost words) so I can get a bit more idea because I don’t want to end up in design if I like (?) on site. I’d like to be in an office as well. I also like some money really, so I can save up and not be so – in my last two years – so worried about things.

**Interviewer —** Really?

**Subject —** I’ll be bored mentally – it’s like now I’ve got so much work on I don’t have time to think about anything. It’s really good – I love being busy. But I think the problem is it’s just all day – you come home in the evening and you’ve got nothing to think about, you know what I mean? Like doing your work – trying to keep learning stuff – like, that can be a problem for me. I get bored easily so…I try and keep myself occupied. I’m also tired – you get really tired. You feel really funny you know. But the good things will be learning more – just getting more practical experience really.
