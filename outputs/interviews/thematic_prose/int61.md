# Interview INT61 — thematic prose

## Route into engineering — why they chose it, and who influenced them
*Coded variables informed by this theme (0): none*

**Interviewer —** Yeah, OK. How do you feel about going back to uni when you finish the placement?

**Subject —** Scared. Because I’ve seen a lot of my mates, like when I see them out at the weekend and stuff, and they’re saying, oh it’s horrible, like the last year’s just so difficult, so it’s a bit scary because although I’m working like a full day every day here, I’m not doing anything in the evenings and they’re all saying that they’ve been getting up at like 6 every day to do work and working all the way through and I’m just like, oh no. Yeah, it just sounds – I don’t know, I think is it worth it sometimes because, like afterwards I don’t particularly know if I want to be a designer afterwards because it is so much hard work and I just think, oh that last year is just going to be horrendous, and I could just come like into a job like this that’s like, it’s not really really difficult but it’s like quite difficult and you don’t necessarily need a really really good degree in design to do it, so.

**Interviewer —** So do you think that your career ambitions have changed from doing the placement or is that how you felt about design before you started?

**Subject —** I think I pretty much felt like that about design because that’s why I came to somewhere that wasn’t as designery. I didn’t like apply for any design consultancies or anything because I didn’t think I’d be good enough to do it because it is quite difficult to get one. So I think I felt that before I came here, but I think it’s helped because I’ve seen different areas that I could work in. Like I could work in Research & Development or marketing or stuff like that. You don’t need a specific degree to be able to do it, so I think it has helped to make me a little bit more confident about that because I was worrying a bit about, well what will I do if I don’t do design, there’s nothing. But there is, there’s so many different things.

**Interviewer —** OK. Do you think then that more women should be encouraged or enabled to do this sort of work?

**Subject —** Yeah, because I think a lot of people shy away from it, from doing – maybe not this one because it’s not too bad here, but design – I mean, I wouldn’t really like to go into a place where it’s just all blokes and I’m the only woman designer. It’d be a bit intimidating.

## Experience of the engineering degree and learning environment
*Coded variables informed by this theme (0): none*

**Interviewer —** OK. How useful do you think that your degree course has been so far to the work that you’re doing? Have you used any of it?

**Subject —** Yeah, I have a bit, but not loads, not as much as I thought, because I’ve been doing a lot of work just with consumers so I’ve been doing a lot of interviews and stuff like that instead, more than I have been doing designing. But now I’m at like the end of a project so I’m – because I’m trying to re-design the [product] pack, so now I’m at the end of it I’m designing, but most of it was just what the consumers want, just to make little changes because they didn’t want a big change to it. But it has been quite handy in like doing things on computers and stuff because I wouldn’t have had a clue otherwise, so that’s been OK.

**Interviewer —** Right, OK. Is there anything that you wish you had learned before you started the placement?

**Subject —** Um, what do you mean, from my course? (Yeah) Yeah, um. Maybe more general things like essay writing and report writing, because we’ve never done any of that and that was – I’m doing a lot of that now so it’s, I have to keep asking like how they want it doing and stuff because I’m not used to doing any of that, we’re just used to drawing and stuff so it’s not the same. So that would have been useful.

**Interviewer —** Right, what?

**Subject —** Well basically all we got was a talk for about an hour saying, go and find a placement, and then a talk from a woman telling us how to write a CV, and that’s pretty much all we had.

**Interviewer —** Has the placement met your expectations? Is there anything you thought you would achieve but haven’t yet or anything like that?

**Subject —** I think it’s made me a lot more confident, because I have to do like a lot of presentations and stuff. But it’s not as designery as I thought it would be. I don’t think I’m – when I go back I think I’m going to struggle for the first few weeks because I’ve not been doing as much of the stuff that I’ve been doing on my course at all, so… I’ve been trying to do a bit out of the placement because I know that I’m not doing any, or that much of it, so I think, yeah, I was hoping it’d be a bit more related in stuff like drawing and stuff. I’ve not been doing any of that.

## Future career intentions and what matters in a job
*Coded variables informed by this theme (4): carpath, workenv, people, flexible*

**Interviewer —** What do you think are the best and worst things about the placement so far?

**Subject —** Um … oh that’s hard. The best was the Christmas party … No I think the best was the project, the big project that I’ve been given to do. I’ve got to hand that in like next week, so that’s been really good because it’s been quite related – it wasn’t given to me by my manager, someone else asked him if I could do it because they thought it was quite related to what I was doing, because it’s packaging. (Yeah) So I think that’s been the best bit because I probably won’t get another one anything like that for the next half of the year. Worst ... I don’t know, it gets a bit boring sometimes. I’m just sat in front of a computer all day every day and it’s up to me to change it really, to go and do something different, and if I’ve got no work that is different then I just have to sit in front of my computer all day and it gets a bit boring because the people around me are a bit old and … old men, not much to talk about, so I think that’s probably the thing that bothers me the most.

I think I’d be more interested in something in like marketing or something like that instead.
