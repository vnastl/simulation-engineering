## The industrial placement — whether and why to go (or not)
*Coded variables informed by this theme (7): placemen, experien, money, jobprosp, jobdecid, industry, perdevel*

**Interviewer —** Okay. How would you summarise your placement experience so far then?

**Subject —** I’ve really enjoyed it. And before I wasn’t sure like I haven’t been – obviously I’ve only been here six months or so, but before I didn’t know what I wanted to do after I graduate but I’m definitely thinking now that I’ll apply for the graduate training scheme, because I’ve just really enjoyed – I don’t mean necessarily here, but just the whole like structured thing and I feel like I’ve learnt so much anyway I wouldn’t mind going straight into a job and doing a similar thing after university.

**Interviewer —** Okay. What have been the best and worst things about the placement so far?

**Subject —** Um, so the worst thing was the sort of upheaval, expected to move to France or whatever, but the best thing has been I feel like I’ve got real responsibility and my project’s real and I’ve – I’ve been able to travel quite a lot, which I wasn’t even expecting, so… I’ve been to Holland and I’ve been to Germany and, um, I’ve been lots of places around the UK as well, so it’s really nice. It’s like – I feel like I’ve just learnt a lot just from doing that, so…

**Interviewer —** Excellent. Is there anything that you thought you would achieve but haven’t so far?

**Subject —** Um, well, one thing I’ve got to do is my dissertation and I haven’t thought what I’m going to write it on or anything like that, so that’s something I’ve got to do soon, obviously I haven’t done so far. But, um, apart from the whole experience of things I just feel like I’ve learnt so much already I’d quite happily walk out tomorrow and, you know, be happy with what I’ve learnt, because just the whole, just being here and getting involved in everything I think it just – it’s really helped.

**Interviewer —** Okay. Do you think that there are any drawbacks at all to doing a placement?

**Subject —** I really can’t think of any. I mean like some of my friends are in their final – final year now doing my course and she said to me that just the people who come back from placement are just totally separate from everyone else and they just – they just get on, and she’s a really conscientious girl or she’s always the most conscientious person I’ve ever known, she just said that you can just see they’re so used to being in work and stuff they just get on with everything, like they just – they’re totally separate and they don’t mix with the other people because no one can understand – everyone’s going ‘Oh we’re just going back to bed for another two hours and then we’ll start this project’ and they’re having meetings, and she said, ‘Oh, you’re going to find it so useful next year’ and, you know, she said that as someone who would never have done a placement, but she said that to me, so… It just – I can’t – I just feel like it will help me so much if I’m going to go for graduate schemes as well. I feel like I know so much more about business and stuff.

**Interviewer —** Okay. Do you think that you have changed personally as a result of the placement?

**Subject —** Um, probably like some of my skills have changed. Um, like I found when I first started I found it quite hard to like ring up people in other countries and just be like, ‘Oh hi, I'm Rebecca’ and start chatting to them, but you’re just expected to and that was something my first manager sort of gave me feedback on. She was like, ‘You’ve just got to get on with it and ring them up’ and so I think I’m probably a bit more confident and, um, but like socially we’re still students really, we all live together and we still act like students, so it’s kind of – it’s probably like that I haven’t changed much, but, um…
