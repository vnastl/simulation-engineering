## Route into engineering — why they chose it, and who influenced them
*Coded variables informed by this theme (7): interest, knowledg, family, varied, careers, nobody, school*

**Subject —** No, my dad’s a – dad’s a – owns a restaurant and my mum’s a housewife.

**Interviewer —** OK. When and why then do you think that you wanted or decided to study air transport management then?

**Subject —** Erm, well initially I wanted to do business – but then, I’ve always liked air transport, so I thought well, I wanted to be an air hostess before but it’s sort of air related anyway, and engineering didn’t – I didn’t really look at engineering, I looked at air transport. Yeah, I’ve always liked air transport and that’s why I went into it.

**Interviewer —** OK. Did you decide that you wanted to do that, before you chose you’re ‘A’ levels, or after?

**Subject —** I think during my ‘A’ levels – I was looking through the UCAS thing and what courses were available, and then I saw air transport management at [this university]. **Interviewer —** OK, did you have any other influences or any advice that helped you make your mind up – did you have any careers advice?

**Subject —** No. (laughter) I just went for it. I liked the sound of it, and then I looked at the modules, and it looked alright.

**Interviewer —** Appeals yes.

**Subject —** Er, I think it just sounds really exciting when erm – erm I don’t really know. It sounds interesting, I guess. But then we study a bit of everything really, like finance and you know, a bit of logistics, economics, just everything really.

**Subject —** I am pleased that I did because apart from this course, I don’t know what else I’d do. So I think if it wasn’t for this course maybe I probably wouldn’t have gone to University. I didn’t want to do business, I didn’t want to do engineering, you know, sort of like mainly engineering like you know electronics or something like that.
