## Experience of the engineering degree and learning environment
*Coded variables informed by this theme (7): curricul, pleased, Fhelp, interper, groupwk, friends, designwk*

**Interviewer —** Do you think that there are benefits to doing group work?

**Subject —** I enjoy group work because although you’ve got – you’ve got the stress of like other people relying on you – you’ve got – it’s not as stressful because I tend to put a lot more pressure on myself, if I’m doing it independently, because I know what I can do and if it’s not perfect then it annoys me and I will try and get it. I push myself, whereas when I’m in group work, you know that it’s a combination of everybody and there’s less of that pressure on your work. You don’t have as much. You get it to a good standard and when it all comes together it’s – I don’t know it’s like…

**Interviewer —** OK, What do you think have been the best and the worst things about the course so far?

**Subject —** The worst things have been like the amount of work. It’s all pressure driven – lets see how you cope with having so many deadlines. I mean, our course is renowned. When I first got here in freshers week, it said if you find somebody that’s not coming out their room, before you call for help, check if they’re doing an industrial design course. And it’s true – there’s so much work, but it’s good ‘cos it’s made me manage my time a lot better in general things. I juggle things and without realising it’s given me a lot more confidence.

**Interviewer —** What about the best things?

**Subject —** The best things well – I don’t know. I’m sure there are some. I think – because – the best things have been because we have had so much work, they register our lectures and stuff, everyone relies so much on their lecturer for stuff that – well I’ve bonded so much more with everybody – because I see my course mates every day, all day, I’ve got a really good sound group of mates and it’s brilliant and people I live with – they don’t know anybody off their course. They know people in halls but that’s it. But they don’t know – and I tend to know everyone I live with in halls, everyone that’s on my course and it’s good because I think – I don’t know, contact wise I can go out and just go on my own and it would be great.

**Interviewer —** Did you realise that the course did include so much engineering based stuff?

**Subject —** I didn’t, at the time, realise there was so much mechanical – like electrical maths and stuff in the first year, ‘cos I did get quite stressed out there. They let me – they kept that quite quiet. Everyone now and go ha, ha, you have to do all this really hard stuff. No they did try and stress us out, they were like, you think you’re going to have a life? You’re not. And they were right. Seriously, with all this work and you thought, ah, I thought they were just trying to scare me so – but they were quite good.

## The industrial placement — whether and why to go (or not)
*Coded variables informed by this theme (6): placemen, experien, educatio, jobprosp, industry, finalyr*

**Interviewer —** Do you feel really and prepared to go on placement work?

**Subject —** Erm, not really. I sort of do. I feel more ready about going to placement than I do about going into my third year. I keep saying this I keep saying, oh if I don’t get a placement – what am I going to do, m I going to defer a year? Because I don’t feel like I’ve had enough time to practice everything I’ve learnt. Because they give you so much work, what you learn you don’t practice. But then I don’t think I would, if I did have the time. I’d say I did, but I wouldn’t and so, I want that year to help me grow up a little bit I suppose. Now I have some ideas about final projects and dissertations because if someone asked me now, I’d be just like I haven’t actually got a clue. I don’t want to do it. I just want to stay in my second year, it’s much better – become a first year again. But yeah,…

**Subject —** Erm, looking at sort of placements that have got product development. Ah no, because you’d be thinking God, you’d want to accept it just because it was a placement and you’d think I might not get another one but I’m glad I didn’t get it because it was very much like testing [product] and stuff and I was saying hmmm, I don’t really want to spend a year, testing [product]. **Subject —** Er, the worst thing – just it being so daunting, when you first start, ‘cos I’m going to be like the student. Er everyone’s going to be – oh, you’re a student. And it’s just having that break from Uni I suppose. Just getting out into the real world and getting a taster you know – that’s the best thing – definitely.
