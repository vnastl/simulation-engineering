**Interviewer —** What is it then, that appeals to you about engineering?

**Subject —** Erm, maybe it's just – it's really interesting. I look at everything and I want to know how it works. Erm, and with engineering, well they teach you how it works. Erm, basically – it's the jobs you can get with engineering are very exciting and – well, the majority are – and you've got the teams and other people with the same ideas, you know.

**Interviewer —** That's what appeals to you about doing aeronautical engineering, did you ever consider any other type of engineering?

**Subject —** Er, I considered a few types but with aeronautical it's – I liked it because it was so broad, you learnt everything from you know, electrical to mechanical to you know say systems and programming.
