## Experience of the engineering degree and learning environment
*Coded variables informed by this theme (1): groupwk*

**Interviewer —** Like what?

**Subject —** Um at Uni you’re only really there for yourself and to get an education and to come away with a piece of paper, telling everyone else that you’ve reached a certain level. Whereas in the workplace, you are working as a group of people, we’ve all got our common aim.

**Interviewer —** Okay you said that most of the work that you’re doing is team work. How do you think doing team work in the work place compares to doing team work at Uni?

**Subject —** Team working at uni is so artificial. It’s so much. Erm you’re also at Uni trying to juggle other bits of work that are individuals (Yeah) so, some people prioritise their individual work rather than their team work, or the other way around. People’s priorities and that you, you know are different. Whereas at work, everybody’s trying to do the same things, so. And you have to work with the people at work. You can’t choose not to. Whereas at Uni, a lot of people get put in a group and then just decide, ‘Oh I‘m not going do it’, you know ‘I’m just going to do my own thing’ and do nothing as part of the team effort so. At work we have to get on and do it, whereas at Uni you have the choice not to.

**Interviewer —** How its organised needs to improve?

**Subject —** Yeah, it needs to, yeah it needs a change. Cos you have trouble – I was speaking to [name] about this the other day, when he came to visit - we have trouble with er foreign students as well. Cos their culture and mentalities different (Yeah) their way of, thinking’s different. You know a lot of them just don’t participate in group work. So. I think we need to do more of it at uni, and the way in which it’s done needs to be altered.
