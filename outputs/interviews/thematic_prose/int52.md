## The industrial placement — whether and why to go (or not)
*Coded variables informed by this theme (5): placemen, experien, jobprosp, perdevel, apptheor*

**Interviewer —** Ok, do you feel like you have had to face any new issues at all like office politics or dealing with different people like the boss or customers or anything like that?

**Subject —** At the moment, the site I’m working on, the people have been very good to me and all that, but sometimes its hard to get some things considering you are a placement student, getting work like for example like getting something like essentials like maybe getting a computer, there are like ‘hold on you’re a placement student you don’t need this and that this time’ and all that. (Yes) I mean there’s so much bureaucracy to get some things.

**Interviewer —** Ok. And they were supportive of you doing that? Ok. How would you summarise the placement so far then?

**Subject —** Oh the placement has been good so far really and it has given me the experiences that I expected to get.

**Interviewer —** Ok, so it’s met your expectations? Ok. What would you say has been the best and worse things about the placement so far?

**Subject —** Well I mean the placement, let me see the best thing is to put in practice what you learn at university, you get to realise that this is what it is all about, and then the worst thing is (Inaudible) you don’t know what to expect so it would be much better if they give you more guidance and tell you oh this is how the structure is supposed to be and go to this training and all that and that.

**Interviewer —** Yes, yes. But that hasn’t been your experience? OK. Do you think you have changed personally as a result of the placement, like more confident or independent things like that?

**Subject —** Yes I have become more confident, more independent. Learn to make more decisions on my own.

**Interviewer —** Yes, so it has reinforced your ideas. Ok. Do you think that the placement will help you when you start work after you have finished university?

**Subject —** Yes, I hope it will help me because I’ve got a year and a half’s experience so far which will be a very good thing on my CV.
